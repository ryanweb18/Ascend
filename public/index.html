        How to play

        1. Goal: arrange the 10 cards in your rack so they climb from lowest (bottom) to highest (top).
        2. Each round you're dealt 10 random cards. You can't shuffle them around — you can only swap one card at a time.
        3. On your turn, take the top discard or draw from the deck.
          • A discard you take must replace a card in your rack.
          • A deck card can replace a card, or be discarded right away.
        4. The card you replace goes face-up on the discard pile.
        5. The first player whose whole rack climbs in order ascends and ends the round.

        Scoring

        • Ascending: 75 points.
        • Everyone else: 5 points for each card in order counting up from the bottom slot, until the first break.
        • Run bonuses (optional): the round winner earns extra for their longest run of consecutive numbers — 3 in a row +50, 4 +100, 5 +200, 6+ +400.
        • First to the target score (500 by default) wins.

        Deck size: 2 players use cards 1–40, 3 players 1–50, 4 players 1–60.
